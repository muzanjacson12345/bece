    A few useful things to know

    Which study mode should I choose?

    Practice mode explains each answer as you go. Timed mock mode gives you 40 seconds per question across the whole quiz and saves feedback until you finish. Start with practice when a topic is new.

    What is included in the revision library?

    Social Studies and Creative Arts topics organised by JHS level. Each note combines explanations, key points and, where available, comparison tables, memory aids and quick checks. It is a focused revision resource rather than a complete BECE subject collection.

    How do essay answers and scores work?

    Essays are for self-assessment: write your response, then compare it with the practice answer guide. Your drafts are saved in this browser automatically. Quiz percentages describe a practice attempt; they are not an official BECE grade.

    How do EXP and streaks work?

    Log in and every correct answer earns 10 EXP (each question counts once per day). The daily quiz adds bonus EXP, and finishing any quiz each day keeps your streak alive. Your recent scores, bookmarks and essay drafts stay in this browser.
